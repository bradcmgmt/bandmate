New · Live flight tracking

Every flight,
live.

Add a flight number and Bandmate follows it — gate and terminal, delays, takeoff, landing, the baggage belt. Each person sees their own flight at the top of their day. Connections read as one journey, and a tight layover gets flagged before anyone's sprinting through Denver. Times stay local to where they happen, with a tiny zone tag when your phone's on another clock.

New · Guest list lock

Close the list.
Slide to lock.

Band and crew request their guests; you or your management crew approve them in a tap. When the list goes to the box office, slide to lock it — or set a time and it locks itself. After that nobody can add, edit or pull a name, and a phone that hasn't refreshed can't sneak one in. You can still add a late one yourself.
